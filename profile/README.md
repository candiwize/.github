# Candiwize

**Stop applying into silence. Write to the person who hires.**

Candiwize finds roles that fit your CV and the hiring manager behind each one, then writes them a personal email you approve.

### How it works

1. **Match.** Finds live roles that fit your CV and explains why each one fits.
2. **Find the person.** Names the recruiter or hiring manager behind the role, with a verified work email.
3. **Write.** Drafts a personal email with your CV attached and an optional cover letter.
4. **You decide.** Nothing is sent until you approve it, and it goes out from your own Gmail.

### Tools

- [Find Jobs](https://candiwize.com/en/find-jobs): from a job post to the person hiring
- [Spontaneous Apply](https://candiwize.com/en/spontaneous-apply): reach companies with no open post
- [Tailor my CV](https://candiwize.com/en/tailor-my-cv): a CV and cover letter shaped to one offer
- [Applications](https://candiwize.com/en/applications): track what you sent and who opened it

Available in English and French. Free to start, no card needed.
